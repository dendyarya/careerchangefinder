# Affiliate links

Rules for any page that links to a partner who pays a commission (Terms of Use and Disclaimer promise this):

1. Show `<AffiliateNote />` (components/page/AffiliateNote.tsx) **above** the first affiliate link on the page,
   not only in the footer or the Privacy Policy. The FTC expects the disclosure to be clear and placed
   before the reader clicks.
2. Mark every affiliate link `rel="sponsored nofollow"` (Google's rule for paid links) and open it in the
   same tab unless there is a reason not to.
3. Affiliate placement never changes a ranking, a match score or a data table. Recommendations of courses or
   tools sit in their own clearly labelled block.
4. Add each new partner to the "Affiliate links" section of the Privacy Policy only if that partner sets
   cookies or receives data beyond the click.
