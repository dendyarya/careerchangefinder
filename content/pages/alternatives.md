There are several good free career tests and career sites. This page compares Career Change Finder with four well-known options, using only what each site states about itself, so you can pick the one that fits what you need. We checked each site on October 27, 2026; prices and features can change, so check the site itself before you pay for anything.

## My Next Move

[My Next Move](https://www.mynextmove.org/) is the U.S. Department of Labor's own career site. It covers more than 900 careers with tasks, skills and BLS pay, and offers a short version of the O\*NET Interest Profiler. **Choose it if** you want the official government tool. Career Change Finder uses the same O\*NET data and the longer 60-item Interest Profiler form, and also offers career change matches, state pay pages and career lists.

## CareerExplorer

[CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/) offers a free career test that takes about 30 minutes across five modules, with a paid membership for extra tools and guidance. **Choose it if** you want a long, detailed assessment and may want the extra tools of a paid membership. Choose Career Change Finder if you want a shorter test with no sign-up, or want to start from the job you have now.

## Truity

[Truity](https://www.truity.com/test/career-personality-profiler-test) offers the Career Personality Profiler, with 94 questions based on the Holland Code and the Big Five. You get a free summary without giving an email address, and the full report costs a small fee. **Choose it if** you want a detailed paid report that combines interests and personality. Career Change Finder's Big Five test and career test are free in full.

## 123test

[123test](https://www.123test.com/career-test/) has a quick picture-based career test: 15 sets of four activities, about 5 to 10 minutes, based on the Holland Code. **Choose it if** you want the fastest possible test. Choose Career Change Finder if you want to see pay, education and skill gaps for each career with the government source.

## Where Career Change Finder falls short

- It covers U.S. careers and U.S. pay only.
- There are no accounts, so test results are not saved across devices.
- There is no personal coaching. For that, a free [American Job Center](https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/find-american-job-centers.aspx) is a good next step.
- Match scores are our estimate of how much of your experience carries over, not a guarantee. See our [Methodology](/methodology).
