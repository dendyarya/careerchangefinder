## My Next Move alternative

[My Next Move](https://www.mynextmove.org/) is the U.S. Department of Labor's own career site. It covers more than 900 careers with tasks, skills and BLS pay, and offers a short version of the O\*NET Interest Profiler. **Choose it if** you want the official government tool. **Choose Career Change Finder as an alternative if** you want the same O\*NET data with the longer 60-item Interest Profiler form, plus [career change matches](/career-change/tool), [pay by state](/careers) for each career and [jobs similar to](/jobs-similar-to) the one you have now.

## CareerExplorer alternative

[CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/) offers a free career test that takes about 30 minutes across five modules, with a paid membership for extra tools and guidance. **Choose it if** you want a long, detailed assessment and may want the extra tools of a paid membership. **Choose Career Change Finder as an alternative if** you want a shorter test with no sign-up and no paid tier, or want to start from the job you have now and see which careers use the same skills.

## Truity

[Truity](https://www.truity.com/test/career-personality-profiler-test) offers the Career Personality Profiler, with 94 questions based on the Holland Code and the Big Five. You get a free summary without giving an email address, and the full report costs a small fee. **Choose it if** you want a detailed paid report that combines interests and personality. Career Change Finder's Big Five test and career test are free in full.

## 123test

[123test](https://www.123test.com/career-test/) has a quick picture-based career test: 15 sets of four activities, about 5 to 10 minutes, based on the Holland Code. **Choose it if** you want the fastest possible test. Choose Career Change Finder if you want to see pay, education and skill gaps for each career with the government source.

## Where Career Change Finder falls short

- It covers U.S. careers and U.S. pay only.
- There are no accounts, so test results are not saved across devices.
- There is no personal coaching. For that, a free [American Job Center](https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/find-american-job-centers.aspx) is a good next step.
- Match scores are our estimate of how much of your experience carries over, not a guarantee. See our [Methodology](/methodology).

## Which one should you pick?

- **You want the official government tool:** My Next Move.
- **You want a long, detailed assessment and may pay for extras:** CareerExplorer.
- **You want a paid report that combines interests and personality:** Truity.
- **You want the fastest test:** 123test.
- **You want a free test with no sign-up, career change matches and pay by state:** Career Change Finder.

Many people use two: one test to find their interest areas, then a data site to compare pay and training for the careers it suggests.

## Common questions

### Is there a free alternative to CareerExplorer?

Yes. My Next Move, 123test and Career Change Finder all have free career tests. Career Change Finder's test is free in full with no sign-up, and every career it suggests links to BLS pay, education and skills from O\*NET.

### What is a good alternative to My Next Move?

Career Change Finder uses the same O\*NET database as My Next Move. It adds the longer 60-item Interest Profiler, career change matches based on the job you have now, and pay for each career in every state with BLS data.

### Are free career tests accurate?

A career test shows which kinds of work match your interests. It does not predict success in a job. Use the results as a shortlist, then read about the daily tasks, pay and training of each career before you decide. See our [guide to changing careers](/guides/how-to-change-careers).
