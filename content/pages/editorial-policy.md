Career Change Finder publishes news and data studies about U.S. jobs, pay and career change. This page explains who writes them, where the facts come from and how we fix mistakes.

## Who we are

Career Change Finder is an independent website. News articles carry the byline **Career Change Finder News Desk**, the team that runs this site. We are not part of, or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Labor, the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) or any other agency. See [About](/about) for how the site is run and paid for.

## What we cover

- **Official data releases**, such as the BLS Employment Situation (jobs report), the Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS), pay data from the Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics program, and O\*NET updates.
- **Our own research**, such as our [data studies](/research), always with the method and its limits.
- **What it means for career changers**, using the career, skills and pay data that power the rest of the site.

We do not copy or rewrite other outlets' stories. When another publication reports something first, we credit it with a link.

## Sources and fact checks

- Every number in a news article comes from a primary source (an official release or dataset) and links to it.
- Numbers from our own data are checked against the published pages before the article goes live.
- Charts are made by us from the cited data. Each chart says where its data comes from. We do not use stock photos or generated pictures of people or events.
- Release dates and reference periods are always stated, because labor data is revised.

## Use of AI tools

We use AI tools to help draft articles, run calculations and build charts. A person on our team decides what to publish, and every figure is checked against the cited source before publication. AI tools never decide the conclusions of a story.

## Independence

The site is funded by ads and may contain affiliate links. Advertisers and partners have no say in what we cover or what the data says. We do not accept payment for news coverage.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it as soon as we can. Substantive corrections are noted at the end of the article with the date of the change, and the article's "Updated" time changes. Minor fixes, such as typos, may be made without a note.

To report an error, email [contact@careerchangefinder.com](mailto:contact@careerchangefinder.com) with the article link and what is wrong.
